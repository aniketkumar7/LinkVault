# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People saving useful links from across the web who need to quickly organize and retrieve them without interrupting their flow.

## Product Purpose

LinkVault captures links with metadata and helps users keep them in meaningful collections. Success means a saved link is accurately categorized with minimal manual work and stays easy to find later.

## Brand Personality

Focused, helpful, dependable. The interface should feel like a capable personal workspace, not a content feed.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered dashboards, generic category suggestions, decorative motion that slows saving, and destructive actions without clear confirmation.

## Design Principles

- Let automation make a clear, explainable recommendation while leaving the final choice to the user.
- Keep capture and organization in one uninterrupted flow.
- Make collection management easy to discover and safe to use.
- Preserve familiar controls and consistent behavior wherever collections appear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard-operable menus and dialogs, clear labels, and reduced-motion-respecting transitions.
